Incotrans Academy

Academic and Skill Development Wing of Incotrans

Incotrans Academy is the academic and skill-development wing of Incotrans, a professionally managed logistics and shipping company. The Academy is established with a clear objective: to bridge the gap between academic learning and real-world logistics, shipping, and supply chain operations.

Backed by Incotrans’ industry expertise, Incotrans Academy delivers job-oriented education, practical training, and industry-recognized certifications for students, fresh graduates, and working professionals seeking careers in logistics, shipping, freight forwarding, and supply chain management.

Our Vision

To become a leading logistics and shipping training institute that produces skilled, employable professionals aligned with global industry standards.

Our Mission

  • Industry-driven logistics education
  • Practical exposure through live projects and internships
  • National & international skill-development collaborations
  • Enhanced employability through certifications
